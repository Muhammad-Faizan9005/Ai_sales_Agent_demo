Frequently Asked Questions?

What is an SEO Audit Service?

Our SEO Audit Service is a comprehensive evaluation of your website’s current SEO performance. We examine all key elements from technical aspects and on-page optimization to site architecture and backlinks to identify areas of improvement. This audit provides actionable insights that help your website improve its search engine rankings, attract more organic traffic, and create a better user experience.

How long does it take to do an SEO audit?

The timeframe for an SEO audit depends on the size and complexity of your website. Typically, our team can complete a detailed audit within 1 week, allowing time to thoroughly analyze data, assess each component, and compile a customized report with clear recommendations for improvement.

Which is the best tool for an SEO audit?

We use a combination of industry-leading tools to conduct our SEO audits, including SEMrush, Ahrefs, and Google Search Console, among others. Each tool provides unique insights that allow us to fully evaluate your website’s SEO health and performance, ensuring no area is overlooked.

How often should you do an SEO audit?

We recommend performing a comprehensive SEO audit at least twice a year to keep your website aligned with the latest search engine updates and best practices. Additionally, conducting an audit whenever significant changes are made to your site such as redesigns or content overhauls ensures optimal performance and continued growth.

What does an SEO audit cover?

Our SEO audit covers all critical aspects of your website, including technical SEO (site speed, crawlability, mobile-friendliness), on-page SEO (content quality, keyword optimization, metadata), off-page SEO (backlink analysis, domain authority), and user experience (navigation, usability). With our audit, you’ll receive a clear roadmap to fix any issues and elevate your website’s SEO performance.
