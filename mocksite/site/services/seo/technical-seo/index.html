Frequently Asked Questions?

What is SEO in technical terms?

SEO in technical terms refers to the process of optimizing the technical aspects of a website to improve its search engine rankings. This includes optimizing site speed, ensuring proper crawlability and indexing, improving mobile-friendliness, enhancing internal linking, and fixing technical issues that may hinder a site’s performance.

What is the difference between On-page and technical SEO?

On-page SEO focuses on optimizing content, keywords, and metadata within the webpage itself, while technical SEO deals with the backend elements like site structure, coding, and server configuration to ensure that search engines can efficiently crawl and index your site.

How is technical SEO different from non-technical SEO?

Technical SEO involves complex, backend optimizations like improving site architecture, fixing broken links, and ensuring fast load times, while non-technical SEO focuses more on content creation, keyword usage, and link-building strategies.

How do we use technical SEO?

We use technical SEO by auditing your website to identify issues such as slow page load speeds, broken links, and poor mobile optimization. Then, we implement strategies to fix these problems, ensuring search engines can properly index your site and that visitors have a smooth user experience.

Why is technical SEO important for your business?

Technical SEO is crucial because it ensures that your website is search engine-friendly, improves site performance, enhances user experience, and increases your chances of ranking higher on search engines, ultimately driving more organic traffic and business growth.
