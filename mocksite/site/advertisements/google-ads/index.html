Frequently Asked Questions?

What are Google Local Service Ads?

Google Local Service Ads are a powerful tool for businesses to appear at the top of Google search results when potential customers search for local services. These ads are tailored to connect businesses with nearby customers who are actively looking for the specific services you offer. By using Google Local Service Ads, your business can build trust through verified reviews, showcase key information like contact details, and appear in a prime position in local search results.

Is a Google Ads account free?

Creating a Google Ads account is free. However, you will need to set a budget and bid for clicks on your ads to start running campaigns. You only pay when users click on your ads (pay-per-click model), so you have full control over how much you spend. Our expert team can set up and optimize your campaigns to ensure you get the best results for your budget, helping you reach your target audience effectively.

Does Google Ads work without a website?

While having a website is highly recommended to ensure that users can explore your offerings further, Google Ads can still work without one. Google offers options like Google My Business and landing pages within the platform, but for the best performance and user experience, having a dedicated website helps drive higher conversions and improves ad quality.

Do you need SEO for Google Ads?

SEO and Google Ads work together to complement each other, but SEO is not required to run Google Ads. Google Ads can bring immediate results, while SEO helps in organic rankings over time. Combining both can enhance visibility, improve quality scores, and increase conversion rates.

How do Google Ads improve business visibility?

Google Ads increases business visibility by placing your ads in front of highly targeted customers who are actively searching for your products or services. With the right keywords, targeting settings, and ad copy, Google Ads helps your business reach the right audience, resulting in higher traffic, more leads, and better brand recognition.
